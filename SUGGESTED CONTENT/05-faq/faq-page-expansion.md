## Incorporation

### Can foreigners start a company in Hong Kong?
Yes. Hong Kong imposes no nationality or residency requirement on company directors or shareholders. A
foreigner can own 100% of a Hong Kong company and incorporate entirely online through the Companies Registry
e-Registry, typically within [VERIFY: 1–3] business days.

### Can a non-Hong Kong resident be a company director?
Yes. Directors of any nationality and residence are allowed. A company needs at least one individual
director; there is no requirement for a Hong Kong resident director. (The company secretary, however, must
be a Hong Kong resident or a Hong Kong corporate body.)

### Do I need to visit Hong Kong to incorporate?
No. Incorporation is completed remotely: documents are signed electronically and filed through e-Registry.
Some traditional banks may ask for a video or in-person interview when opening the bank account; several
virtual banks onboard fully remotely.

### What documents do foreigners need to register a Hong Kong company?
Typically: passport copy and proof of residential address (within 3 months) for each director and
shareholder, the proposed company name(s), share structure, and Articles of Association (your provider
prepares these). Corporate shareholders need the parent company's incorporation documents.

### How long does Hong Kong company registration take?
E-Registry incorporation usually completes within [VERIFY: 1–3] business days after documents are ready.
Paper filing takes around [VERIFY: 4–7] business days. Bank account opening is the longer pole at
[VERIFY: 2–6] weeks.

### Is there a minimum share capital for a Hong Kong company?
No. There is no statutory minimum share capital; most companies register HK$1–HK$10,000. HK$10,000 divided
into 10,000 ordinary shares is the common default because it simplifies future share transfers.

## Company secretary & compliance

### Are Hong Kong company secretary services mandatory?
Yes. Every Hong Kong company must appoint a company secretary under the Companies Ordinance. The secretary
must be a Hong Kong resident individual or a Hong Kong corporate body; the sole director cannot also be the
company secretary of a single-member company.

### What does a Hong Kong company secretary actually do?
The company secretary maintains statutory registers, prepares and files the annual return (NAR1), arranges
and minutes board/shareholder meetings, files changes of directors, shares and registered office with the
Companies Registry, and monitors compliance deadlines. Leap's service covers all of these —
[LINK: /pages/corporate-secretary.html].

### When is the annual return (NAR1) due?
Within 42 days after each anniversary of the company's incorporation date. Late filing triggers
progressively higher government fees. Leap tracks the deadline and files for you —
[LINK: /pages/annual-return-filing.html].

### What happens if I miss a filing deadline?
Late annual returns incur higher registration fees that scale with the delay, and persistent non-filing can
lead to prosecution of the company and its officers. Late profits tax returns can incur penalties and
estimated assessments from the IRD. If you are already behind, contact a provider immediately to regularise.

### Do Hong Kong companies need an audit every year?
Yes. Every Hong Kong company must prepare audited financial statements annually, regardless of size (the
only exemption is for properly declared dormant companies). The audit is performed by a Hong Kong certified
public accountant. See [LINK: /pages/audit-tax-filing.html].

## Tax & banking

### What taxes does a Hong Kong company pay?
Profits tax is two-tiered: 8.25% on the first HK$2 million of assessable profits and 16.5% on the remainder
(corporations). Hong Kong has no VAT/GST, no capital gains tax, no sales tax and no withholding tax on
dividends. Only Hong Kong-sourced profits are generally taxable (territorial principle). Source: ird.gov.hk.

### What tax incentives apply to foreign-owned Hong Kong companies?
The same incentives apply to all Hong Kong companies regardless of ownership nationality: the two-tier
profits tax rate, offshore (non-HK sourced) profit claims where applicable, no tax on dividends or capital
gains, and deductions for R&D expenditure. [VERIFY current-year incentives against the latest Budget.]

passports and address proofs, and business evidence (contracts, invoices, a short business plan). Traditional
banks take [VERIFY: 2–6] weeks and may require an interview; virtual banks/fintech accounts (e.g. Airwallex,
Statrys) onboard remotely and often faster. Leap prepares the complete bank-ready pack as part of

## Visas & living in Hong Kong

### Do I need a visa to own a Hong Kong company?
No. Ownership requires no visa. You need a visa only if you intend to relocate to Hong Kong to work in or
run the business — typically the Investment as Entrepreneurs visa under the General Employment Policy.

### Is the Hong Kong entrepreneur visa difficult to get?
The Investment as Entrepreneurs visa requires a credible business plan, evidence of financial resources and
a contribution to the Hong Kong economy; there is no fixed minimum investment, but Immigration assesses
viability case by case. Well-prepared applications with real operating substance have the strongest record.
